# Stakeholder report (step 10)

Audience: decision-makers and team members who did not follow the research. Answer the decision first; details after. Synthesis, not a link list — the reader should never have to do the analysis themselves.

## Template (`08-report.md`)

```markdown
# <Topic>: desk research report
Date · Authors · Decision supported

## Answer to the decision
2–4 sentences: what the evidence suggests about the decision, how confident we are, and what would change the conclusion. If the evidence is insufficient, say so and name what's missing.

## Key findings
5–8 findings, each: one-sentence finding · strength [H/M/L] · one-line implication.

## Hypotheses: what changed
Short table: hypothesis · verdict · confidence before → after.

## What surprised us
Exploratory findings and challenged assumptions.

## Recommendations
- What to prototype and test next
- What primary research to run (from gaps)
- What to stop assuming

## Limitations
Missing internal data, markets or segments not covered, inaccessible sources, budget limits. Note that desk research does not replace research with real users.

## Method
Questions investigated, number and types of sources, grading approach, period of research. Link to the workspace files.

## Sources
Numbered list of sources actually used in findings (title, publisher, date, URL, accessed date).
```

Keep it ~3–5 pages. Put full evidence cards in `05-evidence.md`, not in the report.
